# Opportunity: Reduce the number of rejected submissions

**Scenario:** B · ICA passport applications

All quotes and metrics are synthetic and labelled as made up.

## Problem Framework

### 1. Goal

Success passport renewals for people who are requesting submitting

### 2. Problem

Comprehension and understanding of requirements in order to proceed successfully

### 3. Context

Citizens who enter the passport renewal site and tries it the first or multiple times

### 4. Constraints

ICA officers approves the renewal

### 5. Success

Success when submission return rates are less than 17%

### 6. Explore

Would users be able to understand what to submit for passport renewal

## Evidence (synthetic)

### User quotes

- as a parent, i don't understand how do i successfully renew my child's passport
- as a parent, i find myself so confused with the instructions on the site.
- as a user renewing my passport, i don't know what's needed during the time of application and how a completed application is suppose to be

### Metrics

- application success rate
- rejected fields/ error rates within field
- Engagement rate on instructions (eg. media/ video) shown to users
- form completion rate
- failed application

## Hypothesis

users will understand instructions for passport renewal when it's a step by step guidance

- Risk type: Usability (build a clickable mockup first)
- Kill switch: users continue to face errors before submission
- Kill-switch signal: high error rates on each page during submission
- Success signal: 10% of returned passport renewal submission by 20 nov
